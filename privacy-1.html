Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how Free PDF Tools handles information when you use this website.

Files you choose

The PDF tools on this page are designed to process selected files in your browser. The website does not intentionally upload your selected documents to a file-processing server. Processing may use your device's memory and browser capabilities. Please avoid using this site for highly sensitive documents if you are unsure about your device or browser environment.

Third-party libraries

This website loads software libraries from third-party content delivery networks (CDNs) to perform some functions. Those providers may receive ordinary technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests library files. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

Advertising

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are added and approved, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. Advertising is not active merely because an empty ad space appears on this page. You can review Google's advertising settings at Google Ads Settings.

Analytics

We do not currently describe or promise any analytics service on this page. If analytics are added in the future, this policy should be updated to identify the service and explain relevant choices.

Children

This website is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes
